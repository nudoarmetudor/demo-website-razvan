Cognitive & Decision Science

Executive Function Under Cognitive Pressure

Overview & Hypotheses

In competitive mind sports like chess, decision-making happens under strict time pressure (e.g., blitz vs. classical time controls). This paper explores how cognitive inhibition and working memory update loops allow expert decision-makers to suppress tempting sub-optimal choices ("blunders") under stress.

Applications in clinical and educational contexts include developing focus strategies for students experiencing test anxiety or cognitive overload during academic evaluations.

Stress Regulation Model

Managing Emotional Tilt in High-Stakes Environments

Physiological & Psychological Coping

When a mistake occurs during a competition or an exam, emotional "tilt" threatens subsequent decision-making. Through structured breathing, diaphragmatic pacing, and cognitive cognitive reframing, individuals can restore heart-rate variability and retain composure.

Psychoeducation Guide

Growth Mindset & Learning Framework

Post-event analysis (whether reviewing a chess game or an academic exam) converts perceived failure into actionable feedback. This guide provides step-by-step methods for students to reflect without self-criticism.
